HOLY SPIRIT

Heavenly Father, my Lord and Savior Jesus and Blessed Holy Spirit; Thank You for allowing me at this moment to enter, and remain in a greater understanding and awareness of Your Glorious, Glorious Presence.

Lord Jesus, I thank you that one of the benefits for me of Your crucifixion, which temporally temporarily cost You everything, is that it has enabled me to take up my cross. My understanding of "taking up my cross" means that I can now reckon, count on and act upon the fact that my old nature is ALREADY dead. Because I decided to follow You Jesus, my old nature has been co-crucified with You. of that fact 11m free to enjoy Your Presence. Any tasks that You have remaining for me in this world, can be accomplished by You, through me, with the new me as one of Your multitude, a member of Your army of living, breathing, moving tabernacles.

Heavenly Father I claim only, only, because of the Blood Covering (that You provided me) and despite of my unworthy thoughts, words and actions, that I still retain the right like Moses and Isaiah, to live in an ever growing awareness of Your Holy, Holy, 3X transcendently Holy Presence LEARNING daily how to please You more and more.

Holy Spirit, I pray that you continue to crowd out and drown out the remaining patterns of myself with Yourself which then allows Your fruit then to ripen, effervesce and emanate from my life out to a parched world.

Specifically:
Love vs selfishness,
Joy vs happiness,
Peace vs anxiety and conflict,
Patience vs frustration,
Kindness vs anger and meanness,
Goodness vs evil intentions,
Faithfulness vs faithlessness
Gentleness vs aggression, and Your control of me Holy Spirit vs my old patterns being in control.

And all these fruits in ever increasing measure...

Because it's when 11m resting in Your indwelling control of me Holy Spirit that 11m moved beyond the results of redirection, (l cannot be pushed, poked, prodded positioned, punished, or in any other way pressured or guilted into Your Presence).

Because it's when 11m resting in Your indwelling control of me Holy Spirit that 11m moved beyond the results of reformation because my stinking, stubborn old patterns of behavior are just not reformable.

No Holy Spirit, it's when 11m resting in Your indwelling control of me that 11m moved instead into the realm of transformation of my self-centered mind, will, emotions and personality.

Lord Jesus, Savior of this fallen world, I now bow this physical body of mine before You, (which unfortunately also includes it's earthly appetites, it's desires and it's defects and weaknesses) but I bow it before You anyway as an act of worship, especially in light of the fact that it's Your body that still bears the marks intended for me as my just punishment.

Holy Spirit, Sanctifier and Guide of the people of God, I gratefully resubmit my mind, will, emotions and personality. But those are concepts. More specifically, intimately and personally Holy Spirit, I surrender to You my thoughts; I surrender my desires (please help me replace my desires with Your Desires), I surrender to You my feelings and how I interact with others within my orbit. In essence Holy Spirit, I offer You all of me at this moment as an act of worship.

YHWH I acknowledge You as the Designer, Creator, Sustainer and Ruler of this vast, vast 13.8 billion light year wide universe. Accordingly I prostrate my all spiritually in absolute reverent worship before Your Splendor, Your Majesty and Your Magnificence.

I ascribe ALL Praise, ALL Worship, ALL Glory, ALL Honor (every iota of these in the universe) I long to be returned to You Father, and to Your Son and to Your Holy Spirit. And very soon they all will be returned to You.

Thank you Devine, Blessed and Glorious Three in One, I so need and depend upon Your mercy directed toward me.

And it's in Jesus's NAME, Yeshua my Hamashiach, and Your name only Jesus do I dare to approach. Thank You, Thank You, Thank You
